## Math

The Michaelis–Menten rate law is $v = \frac{V_{max}[S]}{K_m + [S]}$, or as a display equation:

$$
\frac{d[S]}{dt} = -\frac{k_{cat}\,[E]_0\,[S]}{K_m + [S]}
$$
